# Worst Hotel Ever — Economy System

## Purpose

Money should create decisions, not accounting work.

## Core Loop

Guests  
→ Revenue  
→ Costs / Problems / Compensation  
→ Day Result  
→ Upgrades / Repairs / Expansion

## Revenue

Primary:

- Room payment

Optional MVP:

- Coffee / simple paid extras

Later:

- Amenities
- Events
- premium services

## Price

MVP:

- Cheap
- Standard
- Expensive

Price affects:

- demand
- revenue
- expectations
- value perception

## Costs

- Compensation
- Major repairs
- Restock
- Upgrades
- Expansion

Later:

- operating costs
- maintenance
- amenity costs

## Minor Repairs

Usually cost time more than money.

Major failures may cost money.

## Supplies

MVP:

basic supplies auto-restock between days with aggregated cost.

## Shared Cash

One Hotel balance.

Avoid individual wallets.

## Upgrade Categories

- Operational
- Capacity
- Service
- Quality

## Good Upgrade Rule

Upgrade should change gameplay.

Examples:

- Second Toolbox
- Luggage Cart
- Better Linen
- Coffee Machine
- New Room

## Expansion

More capacity means:

- more revenue potential
- more workload
- more risk

## Bankruptcy

No hard bankruptcy loop.

If cash collapses:

- small debt
- restricted spending
- recovery gameplay

## Main Spending Timing

Mostly during Preparation.

## MVP Economy

Revenue:
- room stays
- optional coffee

Costs:
- compensation
- major repair
- restock
- upgrades
- new rooms

## Core Principle

Money exists to force prioritization between stability, efficiency, quality and growth.
