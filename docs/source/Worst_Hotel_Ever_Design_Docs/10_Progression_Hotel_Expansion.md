# Worst Hotel Ever — Progression & Hotel Expansion

## Philosophy

Progression should be physical and systemic, not mostly numerical.

## Growth Dimensions

- Rooms
- Floors
- Wings
- Amenities
- Service Areas
- Equipment
- Room Types
- Infrastructure

## Expansion vs Upgrade

Expansion:

adds capacity / area / systems.

Upgrade:

improves existing workflow or capability.

## More Rooms

Benefit:

- revenue
- capacity

Responsibility:

- cleaning
- repair
- service
- travel
- guest load

## Player Choice

Allow over-expansion.

Player may choose:

- small high-quality Hotel
- large budget Hotel
- mixed strategy

## Room Categories

Should emerge from:

- size
- quality
- amenities
- capacity
- furniture

## Amenities

Every Amenity adds:

- benefit
- demand
- possible workload
- breakdowns
- maintenance

Examples later:

- Restaurant
- Laundry
- Parking
- Pool
- Bar
- Gym
- Spa
- Conference

## Distance Is Difficulty

Growth increases travel.

Infrastructure / Storage / Shortcuts counter it.

## Unlocking

Use:

- money
- reputation
- rating
- room count
- prerequisite spaces / systems

Avoid day-number gating.

## Rating

Can gate broader progression without ending the game.

## Pre-authored Expansion

Prefer fixed expansion slots over free construction.

## Soft Maximum

Rough future target:

~20–30 rooms before progression shifts toward quality, specialization and events.

## Complexity Can Be Opt-In

Example:

Before Laundry unlock:
linen restock abstracted.

After Laundry:
physical loop appears.

## Area Closure

Allow closing rooms / wings to reduce workload.

## MVP Progression

Start:

- 4 rooms
- one Toolbox
- basic linen

Upgrade pool:

- second Toolbox
- Luggage Cart
- better linen
- Coffee Machine
- better Bed
- TV
- Room 5
- Room 6

## Alpha

- Floor 2
- Laundry
- room categories
- first major Amenity
- distributed storage
- premium rooms

## Core Principle

Every major upgrade should answer:

**What benefit do we get?**  
**What new responsibility does it create?**
