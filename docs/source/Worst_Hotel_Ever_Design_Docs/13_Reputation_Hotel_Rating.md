# Worst Hotel Ever — Reputation & Hotel Rating

## Separation

### Reputation
Dynamic public opinion.

### Hotel Rating
More structural / formal class.

### Identity Tags
What the Hotel is known for.

Do not merge them.

## Reputation Sources

- Reviews
- Guest Satisfaction
- Complaints
- Recovery
- major Events

## Reviews

Generated after checkout from:

- Room quality
- Cleanliness
- Requests
- Problems
- Complaint response
- Compensation
- Waiting
- Value

## Review Topics

Examples:

- Cleanliness
- Service
- Value
- Comfort
- Problem Recovery
- Waiting

## Recovery Matters

One problem should not always destroy a stay.

Quick resolution can strongly soften the review.

## Recent Weighting

Recent reviews should matter more.

Reputation can recover.

## MVP Reputation

Simple:

- Poor
- Average
- Good

plus 1–5 star reviews.

Reputation affects Demand.

## Hotel Rating

Possible ★ levels.

Slow-changing.

Based on:

- scale
- quality
- amenities
- cleanliness
- reputation

MVP safe to defer full Rating to Alpha.

## Identity Tags

Later:

- Family Friendly
- Business Friendly
- Budget
- Luxury
- Quiet
- Party
- Great Food

Should emerge from sustained Hotel behavior and facilities.

## Core Principle

Reputation says **how Guests currently feel about us**.  
Rating says **what level of Hotel we structurally are**.
