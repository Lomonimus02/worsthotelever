# Worst Hotel Ever — Guest-to-Guest Interaction

## Philosophy

Avoid full social simulation.

Guests influence each other mostly through shared environment.

## Interaction Categories

- Noise
- Shared Space
- Queue
- Property Conflict
- Room Conflict
- Social Conflict later

## Noise

Noise has:

- source
- radius / local exposure
- duration
- intensity

Guest reaction depends on:

- tolerance
- time
- room location
- traits

## Reception Queue

One slow Guest affects others through waiting.

## Congestion

Shared spaces can become crowded.

This creates:

- slower movement
- annoyance
- visual chaos

## Wrong Baggage

Physical luggage can create ownership mistakes.

## Wrong Room

Assignment / access mistakes can create Guest conflict.

## Group Behavior

Groups move / arrive in related patterns.

No full pairwise social AI needed.

## Local Signals

Guest reacts to environment signals:

- Noise
- Congestion
- Queue Wait
- Ownership conflicts

This scales better than Guest A simulating Guest B relationships.

## Shared Problems

One root cause may affect several Guests.

Avoid duplicate repair tasks.

## MVP

- Noise
- Reception Queue
- simple congestion
- wrong baggage
- wrong room

## Core Principle

Guests should collide through shared Hotel systems, not through expensive social simulation.
