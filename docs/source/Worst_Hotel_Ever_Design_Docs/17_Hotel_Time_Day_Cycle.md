# Worst Hotel Ever — Hotel Time & Day Cycle

## Purpose

Time creates rhythm and operational pressure.

## Day Length

Target active Day:

~15–25 minutes.

First onboarding Day may be shorter.

## Day Phases

Conceptually:

- Early Morning
- Morning
- Midday
- Afternoon
- Evening
- Night
- End / Close

Do not make phases rigid scripted missions.

## Morning

Typical:

- Check-outs
- dirty Rooms
- linen
- luggage
- Reception traffic

## Turnover Window

Players prepare Rooms before arrivals.

## Arrivals

Reservations have arrival windows, with slight variation.

## Walk-ins

Distributed through day.

## Evening

Often more requests / noise / complaints.

## Night

New arrivals reduce.

Noise sensitivity may increase.

## End Day

Soft ending:

- stop new arrivals
- reduce random pressure
- let Players resolve tails
- allow End Day

## Overnight Guests

Persist across days.

Overnight simulation simplified.

## Problems

Persistent problems remain.

Some can worsen cautiously.

## Preparation

After Summary:

- forecast
- upgrades
- pricing
- repairs
- room prep
- next-day planning

## Timers

Avoid arbitrary countdowns.

Pressure comes from:

- patience
- arrival windows
- room readiness
- escalation

## Calendar

Weekdays / seasons later.

## MVP Rhythm

Morning Check-outs  
→ Turnover  
→ Afternoon Arrivals  
→ Evening Rush  
→ Night  
→ Summary / Preparation

## Core Principle

The clock should create rhythm, not constantly scream at the Player.
