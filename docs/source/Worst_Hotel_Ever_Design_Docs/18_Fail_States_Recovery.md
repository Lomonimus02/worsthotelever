# Worst Hotel Ever — Fail States & Recovery

## Philosophy

Failure should create gameplay, not frequent Game Over.

## Local Failure

Examples:

- unhappy Guest
- bad Review
- Room Out of Service
- missed request
- damaged equipment

## Crisis

Several failures cascade:

- too many broken Rooms
- low Cash
- low Reputation
- overload

Still should usually be recoverable.

## No Instant Game Over For

- zero Cash
- bad Reputation
- lost Guest
- failed Event
- multiple dirty Rooms

## Debt

Prefer small debt / deficit over bankruptcy.

Positive profit repays it.

Debt may restrict expensive upgrades.

## Reduced Operation

Player can recover by:

- closing Rooms
- closing Wings
- disabling Amenities
- lowering Price
- rejecting Walk-ins
- repairing
- serving fewer Guests well

## Low Reputation Recovery

Lower reputation may naturally lower Demand.

This gives breathing room.

## Rating

More stable than Reputation.

Potential downgrade later, not instant collapse.

## Emergency Closure

Later possible:

temporarily close a zone / Hotel.

Consequences:

- refunds
- lost revenue
- reputation hit

Benefit:

- recovery space

## Guest Failure

Bad outcome means:

- compensation
- review
- early leave
- lost revenue

Not “mission failed”.

## Persistent Consequences

Damage and financial problems can remain into later Days.

## Difficulty Assistance

Director reduces optional new pressure during serious crisis.

It does not magically erase existing problems.

## MVP

- unhappy Guests
- bad Reviews
- OOS Rooms
- financial loss
- Reputation decline
- small debt
- recovery tools

## Core Principle

A bad Day should make the next Day interesting, not erase the Save.
