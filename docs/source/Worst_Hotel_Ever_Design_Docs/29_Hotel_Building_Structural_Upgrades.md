# Worst Hotel Ever — Hotel Building & Structural Upgrades

## Purpose

Defines how the physical Hotel itself grows.

## Main Rule

Structural Upgrade must meaningfully change at least one:

- Capacity
- Workflow
- Hotel Capability

## Furnishing vs Structure

Furnishing:
what exists inside current space.

Structural:
what spaces / routes exist at all.

## Expansion vs Upgrade

Expansion:
adds new usable area.

Structural Upgrade:
improves topology / capacity of existing building.

## No Free Construction

Do not build:

- wall editor
- custom plumbing
- free corridor drawing
- arbitrary stair placement

Use pre-authored expansion zones.

## Growth Stages

Possible progression:

### Small Motel
- Lobby
- 4 Rooms
- Storage
- Utility

### Expanded Motel
- 6–8 Rooms
- better Storage
- larger Reception

### Small Hotel
- Floor 2
- Elevator
- Laundry
- room variety

### Developed Hotel
- Wings
- Amenities
- stronger infrastructure

### Specialized Hotel
- Premium Wing
- Conference
- Restaurant
- etc.

## Room Expansion

Closed Room  
→ purchase  
→ usable Room

MVP Rooms 5–6 can come pre-furnished to minimum standard.

## Floor Expansion

Adds:

- capacity
- travel
- cleaning
- infrastructure
- service load

Major progression milestone.

## Wings

Support specialization:

- Family
- Premium
- Business

Prefer emerging identity from furnishing / placement.

## Lobby Expansion

Can physically add:

- queue space
- seating
- second Reception
- luggage area
- coffee area

## Reception Expansion

Example:

- 1 station
- 2 stations
- larger Front Desk

Parallelism increase, not abstract percentage.

## Storage Expansion

- Larger Storage
- Floor Linen Closet
- Tool Station
- distributed logistics

## Staff Shortcuts

Real route shortening.

Strong upgrade because benefit is felt physically.

## Service Corridors

Later:

- Guests use public routes
- Players use both

## Elevators

Progression:

- Stairs only / one Elevator
- better capacity
- second Elevator
- Service Elevator

## Connectivity Upgrades

Possible later:

- connecting bridge corridor
- extra stair
- Staff Door

## Utility Room

Can expand with:

- panels
- boiler later
- generator later

## Laundry

Structural upgrade that replaces abstract linen loop with physical system.

## Amenity Spaces

Expansion slots can become:

- Restaurant
- Conference
- Gym

Meaningful specialization.

## Conversion

Later convert space with:

- cost
- downtime

## Renovation

MVP:

instant between days.

Later:

temporary closure / construction visuals.

## Exterior

Later:

- Entrance
- Sign
- Canopy
- Parking
- Pool
- Garden

## Area States

- Active
- Closed
- Under Renovation
- Out of Service

## Manual Closure

Players may close zones to reduce workload.

## Demand

Structure affects Demand through actual capacity / amenities / room types.

## Workload

Expansion always increases some combination of:

- travel
- Guests
- cleaning
- repair
- queues
- infrastructure load

## Infrastructure Bottlenecks

Growth should reveal:

- one Elevator no longer enough
- Storage too far
- Lobby too small

creating reasons for later upgrades.

## Player Count

Large Hotel may be closed partially if fewer Players are online.

## Expansion Preview

Show:

- Cost
- Capacity
- Requirements
- New Systems
- Workload effect

## Branching Growth

Avoid single linear route.

Example:

Floor 2  
or  
Lobby + Laundry

## Small Hotel Viability

Player should be able to remain small and improve quality.

## Space As Resource

Limited expansion slots create decisions.

## MVP

Start:

- Lobby
- Storage
- Utility
- Rooms 1–4

Unlock:

- Rooms 5–6

Potential:

- Reception improvement
- small Storage upgrade

## Alpha

- Floor 2

- Staff Shortcut
- Larger Lobby

## Core Principle

**More Space → More Opportunity → Longer Routes → More Systems → More Responsibility**
