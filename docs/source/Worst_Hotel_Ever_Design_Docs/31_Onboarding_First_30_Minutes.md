# Worst Hotel Ever — Onboarding / First 30 Minutes

## Goal

Teach the logic of the Hotel, not a list of buttons.

By ~30 minutes Players should understand:

- Check-in
- Room assignment
- Luggage
- Requests
- Cleaning
- Room Ready
- Repair
- Leak / Wet Floor
- Complaints
- Check-out
- Reviews
- Money
- Persistence
- Preparation
- first Upgrade

## No Separate Tutorial Level

First Hotel is the real Save.

Everything remains:

- money
- room state
- problems
- upgrades
- reviews

## Controlled Opening

First 15–20 minutes may use controlled conditions:

- easy first Guest
- one Ready Room
- first Breakdown = Sink Leak
- Director heavily limited

After basics, normal simulation takes over.

## Starting Hotel

- Lobby
- Reception
- 4 Rooms
- Linen Storage
- Tool Rack
- Trash Disposal
- Utility
- locked Room 5–6
- future upper-floor access

## Starting Equipment

- 1 Toolbox
- 1 Mop
- basic Linen
- Towels
- simple Coffee source later
- no Luggage Cart

Player must feel the problem before buying the solution.

## Start

Players spawn in Lobby.

Can move, explore, bump into each other.

Then choose:

**Open Hotel**

## First Guest

- Patient
- simple Reservation
- one Suitcase
- no unusual Requirements

Teaches:

- Reception
- Room assignment
- Guest physical movement

## First Luggage

Suitcase remains in Lobby.

Player physically delivers it.

Creates first natural co-op split.

## Second Guest

Arrives while first Guest is already in Room.

Shows Hotel does not wait for one task to finish.

## First Request

Guest requests Extra Towels.

Teaches:

- Request
- Storage
- physical delivery
- source-based completion

## First Dirty Room

One Room may begin:

Vacant + Not Ready

with:

- Used Bed
- Dirty Towels
- Trash

Upcoming Reservation creates reason to prepare it.

## First Turnover

Players clean in parallel.

Room becomes Ready from world conditions.

## First Natural Consequence

If Room is not Ready when Guest arrives:

Guest waits.

No Mission Failed.

## First Breakdown

After basic service is understood:

Sink Leak.

## Leak Flow

Sink leaks  
→ Wet Floor  
→ Guest may notice  
→ Toolbox needed  
→ Repair stops source  
→ Mop removes consequence

Most important lesson:

**Repair Source ≠ Remove Consequences**

## First Complaint

If response is slow:

Complaint may escalate.

Fast recovery can repair Guest impression.

## Workload Overlap

Next:

- Reception Guest
- Towels
- dirty Room
- luggage
- Leak aftermath

No tutorial instruction telling Players exact priority.

They decide.

## First Queue

Two arrivals close together.

Introduces:

- waiting
- patience
- Impatient Trait

## Trait Learning

Teach through behavior, not stat sheet.

## First Check-out

Player checks out Guest.

Guest pays.

Leaves physically.

Room remains Not Ready.

## First Review

Contextual review may mention:

- quick recovery
- slow check-in
- room condition

## Mini-Rush

Later in Day 1:

- 2 arrivals
- 1 dirty Room
- 1 Request
- maybe Broken TV

Broken TV teaches non-urgent Problem.

## First Day End

New arrivals stop.

Players can finish work.

End Day.

Unfixed state persists.

## Day Summary

Show:

- Revenue
- Costs
- Reviews
- Reputation
- 1–3 key reasons

## First Preparation

Show:

- Tomorrow Reservations
- Room state
- Repairs
- Price
- Upgrade choice

## First Upgrade Choice

Examples:

- Second Toolbox
- Luggage Cart
- Better Linen
- Coffee Machine

Only enough money for one / maybe two.

Upgrade should solve a pain Players already felt.

## Physical Appearance

Purchased upgrade appears in Hotel.

## Day 2

Proves Hotel changed because of yesterday’s decision.

May introduce:

- Walk-in
- Messy Guest
- higher occupancy

Noise can wait until later Day 2.

## Do Not Introduce Yet

- Hotel Rating
- Identity Tags
- Large Events
- Tourist Bus
- major Power Outage
- Floor 2
- advanced furnishing
- complex Infrastructure
- Debt
- Maintenance

## Teaching Structure

For each mechanic:

**Show → Ask → Complicate**

First encounter is calm.

Later overlap creates chaos.

## Director Handoff

After Players have:

- Checked in
- completed Request
- prepared Room
- resolved Breakdown

onboarding protections can lift.

## Hints

Escalate:

1. world cue
2. icon
3. short text

Only if Player appears stuck.

## Multiplayer

Never assign:

Player 1 = Receptionist.

Shared state only.

## First Day Length

~12–18 minutes.

Day 2 closer to normal.

## Condition-Based Timing

Do not trigger tutorial beats by exact real-time minute.

Use progression conditions.

## Skip

Returning Player can skip guided opening.

## Success Criteria

By ~30 min, Players can independently:

- serve Guest
- find Room
- deliver luggage
- understand Not Ready
- bring supplies
- use Tools
- fix simple Problem
- interpret Complaint
- choose priorities
- buy Upgrade

## Emotional Criteria

Players should have:

- divided work
- interrupted each other
- physically collided
- solved a small disaster
- laughed at a mistake
- seen yesterday’s consequences

## Core Principle

**One System at a Time → Systems Overlap → Players Take Control → Chaos Becomes Emergent**
